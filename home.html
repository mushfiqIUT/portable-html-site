The Online Market Research database provides details of market research reports published by ITC and other international, regional or national organizations, including trade promotion organizations, import promotion offices and research institutions. It aims to offer trade support institutions in developing countries and economies in transition easy access to online market reports of particular relevance to small and medium enterprises.
